# Screenshot Plan

Google Play phone screenshots should be portrait, without device chrome, status bar, or navigation bar. Recommended export size: **1080 × 1920 px** or **1440 × 2880 px**.

## Recommended Phone Screenshots
1. **Home Dashboard**
   - Show the premium tool grid and privacy badge.
   - Caption: `10 premium woodworking calculators. 100% offline.`

2. **Board Optimizer**
   - Show stock length, kerf, multiple cuts, and material efficiency.
   - Caption: `Optimize cut lists with kerf and waste built in.`

3. **Board Feet & Cost**
   - Show thickness, width, length, waste, price, and total cost.
   - Caption: `Estimate lumber volume, waste, and purchase cost.`

4. **Equal Spacing**
   - Show baluster/slat spacing with center marks.
   - Caption: `Lay out balusters, slats, screws, and shelves evenly.`

5. **Local History / Privacy**
   - Show saved calculation history.
   - Caption: `Save results locally. No ads. No tracking. No internet.`

## Tablet Screenshots
If distributing to tablets/Chromebooks, capture 2-3 landscape or portrait tablet screenshots showing:
- Home dashboard adaptive grid
- Board optimizer details
- Lumber reference / board feet calculator

## Capture Tips
- Use English locale for overseas listings.
- Use light mode for the primary listing set.
- Disable Android status/navigation bars or crop them out.
- Use realistic values that demonstrate usefulness.
- Keep screenshots clean and readable at thumbnail size.
