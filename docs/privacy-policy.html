Privacy Policy

Woodworking Calculator Pro is an Android workshop calculator app. The free tier is fully functional. An optional one-time “Pro” in-app purchase unlocks the advanced calculators. The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no usage tracking.

Offline calculations · No ads · No tracking

Effective date: May 9, 2026

Information We Collect

Woodworking Calculator Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not require account registration and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or device identifiers.

Local Calculation History

If you choose to save a calculation, the saved result is stored locally on your device so you can view it later inside the app. This history stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party. You can delete saved history entries inside the app at any time.

Network Access

The app does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. All calculations run on the device. The only network-adjacent surface is the Google Play Billing Library, which communicates with the Google Play app on the same device through Android inter-process communication (IPC) when you tap “Unlock Pro” or “Restore purchase”. The app itself opens no sockets and uploads no calculation, usage, or personal data.

Permissions

Woodworking Calculator Pro does not request sensitive Android permissions. Clipboard actions only occur when you explicitly tap the copy button to copy calculation results for your own use.

Third-Party Services and SDKs

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, social SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs. The only third-party library that touches anything outside the app process is the Google Play Billing Library, used solely to process the one-time “Pro” purchase. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play under Google’s own privacy policy.

Payments

Woodworking Calculator Pro is free to download. The optional one-time “Pro” unlock is sold through Google Play’s in-app purchase system. Payment processing, purchase records, taxes, refunds, and related account information are handled by Google Play under Google’s policies. The app itself does not process or store payment information; the only on-device record we keep is a single boolean “Pro unlocked” flag in app-private storage, which is rebuilt from Google Play on every cold start.

Children's Privacy

The app is a utility tool for woodworking planning and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no server-side user database for this app. Saved calculation history is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's normal operating system security controls.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved calculation history remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes app-local data according to Android system behavior.

International Users

The app is intended for users in international markets. Since the app does not collect personal data, no personal data is transferred internationally by the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. If a future version introduces any data collection or network-based feature, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure should be updated before release.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer using the support email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
